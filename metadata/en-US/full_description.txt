<b>A private, personal web environment for Android</b>

Cat Browser is an independent privacy-focused browser for Android built on Mozilla's Gecko Engine and Mozilla Android Components. It offers a fully-featured web browser with extension support, enhanced by powerful privacy features, local AI assistance (opt-in), and advanced tab organization tools.

<b>Spaces with Their Own Rules</b>

Cat Browser shields you from third-party surveillanceâ€”whether by ISPs, tracking companies, or other entities seeking to monitor your browsing habits:

- <b>Web Engine Hardening</b>: Comprehensive privacy and security presets to resist tracking and fingerprinting
- <b>Cookie Isolation</b>: Cookies separated by container or context, preventing cross-site tracking
- <b>Content Blocking</b>: Blocks ads, trackers, and unwanted content by default
- <b>Tor Proxying</b>: Route browsing traffic through the Tor network for enhanced anonymity
- <b>Local Search Engine</b>: Your device handles search and retrieval, minimizing remote service contact
- <b>No Tracking or Telemetry</b>: Cat Browser does not track, monitor, or collect any browsing data

WebLibre keeps a full-text index of the pages you visit, on your device. Type a word you remember from an article, recipe, or forum thread, and it finds the page even if that word never appeared in its title or address. Tabs, bookmarks, history, and saved feed articles appear in the same address bar search, and bang shortcuts such as !w send a search straight to a specific site.

RSS/Atom feeds, Reader Mode, PDF/Markdown/full-page image export, and on-device translation are built in.

<b>Websites as Apps</b>

Install sites you use often on your home screen, where they open in their own window like an app. Choose whether each one shares logins with your browser or a container, or keeps its own, so the same service can be signed in to different accounts. Website notifications work through UnifiedPush (requires a separate delivery app).

<b>Built-in Tor, VPN, and Proxies</b>

Use Tor without a separate app, import a WireGuard VPN configuration, or connect through SOCKS, HTTP, Shadowsocks, and other supported proxy protocols. These connections apply only to WebLibre. Keep everyday browsing on your normal connection while private tabs, isolated tabs, or cookie-isolated containers use Tor, a VPN, or a proxy, or send all regular browsing through one.
